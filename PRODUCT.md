# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Stack

Swift and native iOS APIs.

## Product Purpose

hw_level measures the tilt of flat and upright surfaces with an iPhone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app is free and works offline. It has no ads, tracking, accounts, or purchases. It switches measurement modes automatically. Calibration persists until reset. Sound and haptic feedback are optional and on by default.

## Brand Commitments

Use the hw_ prefix. Use standard Apple interface styling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and indicators that do not depend on color.
